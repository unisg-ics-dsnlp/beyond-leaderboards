The claim rests on a dubious causal story (historical pollution → culture → bottled-water demand) and an unexplained assertion that purchases “help us,” without defining the beneficiaries or the mechanism.  

From a libertarian standpoint the decisive facts are voluntary exchange and property rights: competent adults may buy bottled water if they prefer it, and producers may sell it.
