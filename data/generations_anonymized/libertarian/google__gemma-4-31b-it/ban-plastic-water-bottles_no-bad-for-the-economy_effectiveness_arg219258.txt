While I vehemently oppose banning bottled water—as such a ban is an act of coercion that violates property rights and the principle of voluntary exchange—the author's argumentation is ineffective. 

The author relies on "economic harm" and the loss of corporate profits. From a libertarian perspective, the loss of a company's money is not a moral argument against a ban; the moral argument is that the state has no right to interfere with peaceful, consensual transactions between adults. Appealing to the financial interests of giant corporations lacks moral force and fails to address the fundamental violation of negative liberty.
