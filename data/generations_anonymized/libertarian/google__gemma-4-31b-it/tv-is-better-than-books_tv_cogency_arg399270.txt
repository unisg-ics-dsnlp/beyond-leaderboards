From a libertarian perspective, this argument is fundamentally flawed. First, the claim that actors wouldn't have jobs without TV ignores the principle of **voluntary exchange**; actors would simply find other markets (theater, film, digital media) to offer their labor. 

Second, the claim that we wouldn't know what is happening without TV is a fallacy. Information is a commodity; if there is a demand for news, the market provides diverse delivery mechanisms.
