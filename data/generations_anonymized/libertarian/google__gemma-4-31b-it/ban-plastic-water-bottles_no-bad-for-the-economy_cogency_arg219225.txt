Rating: 2 (Medium)

From a Libertarian perspective, the conclusion—that banning bottled water is a mistake—is absolutely correct because a ban is an act of coercion that violates property rights and voluntary exchange.

However, the author's cogency is only medium because they rely on weak premises. Appealing to "helping the economy" is a collectivist argument; the morality of a product doesn't depend on its GDP contribution, but on the consent of the buyers and sellers. The points about waste and recycling are relevant to externalities, but the primary argument should be the Non-Aggression Principle: the state has no right to prohibit a peaceful transaction between consenting adults.
