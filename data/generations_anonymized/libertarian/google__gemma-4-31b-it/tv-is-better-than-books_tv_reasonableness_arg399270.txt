The argument is fundamentally flawed. First, claiming actors "wouldn't have jobs" ignores the principle of **voluntary exchange**; if demand for TV vanished, labor would shift to other mediums (theater, film, digital) based on market preference, not state necessity.

The author mistakes a current convenience for an essential necessity, failing to recognize that these services are products of private property and voluntary contracts, not indispensable pillars of existence.
