**Rating: 2 (Medium)**

The argument makes a modest emotional appeal by invoking job losses ("hundreds of people without jobs") and economic harm to ordinary Americans ("Millions of Americans use and purchase water bottles everyday"). These are relatable concerns that can trigger anxiety about livelihoods and economic stability.

However, from my perspective, the appeal is **weakened** by its clumsy conflation of worker welfare with corporate profit protection — defending Dasani and Nestlé's bottom line is hardly emotionally compelling to most audiences. The argument undermines itself emotionally by appearing to prioritize corporate interests over genuine human concern.

The emotional resonance is present but diluted — hence a **medium** rating.
