**Rating: 2 (Medium)**

The premise that TV allows you to "explore the world from a couch" has some truth — documentaries and nature programming *can* raise ecological awareness and foster connection to distant ecosystems.

However, from an eco-justice lens, this premise is **partially problematic**:

- It frames **passive consumption** as equivalent to genuine engagement with the natural world — which it is not.
- It subtly reinforces a **disconnection from nature**, treating ecosystems as entertainment rather than living systems we have a **stewardship duty** toward.
- Real ecological literacy and environmental commitment tend to come from **direct experience** with nature, not screen mediation.

The premise is *not entirely false*, but it carries assumptions that sit uneasily with values of genuine ecological engagement and responsibility.
