The argumentation is poorly reasoned from multiple angles:

- **Causal logic is weak**: Attributing 50% of global bottled water consumption purely to "European culture" shaped by historical pollution is an oversimplification. It ignores infrastructure investment, corporate marketing, regulatory failures, and economic factors.

- **"Help us" is dangerously vague**: Who is "us"? This framing obscures accountability entirely — a red flag under the **polluter-pays principle**. If industrial pollution degraded water sources, the responsible parties should remediate those systems, not shift the burden onto consumers buying single-use plastic.

- **Shifts burdens forward**: Normalizing bottled water as a cultural fix rather than addressing root causes of water pollution shifts remediation costs to future generations — a direct violation of **intergenerational justice**.

The argument lacks sufficient evidence, acceptable framing, and relevant solutions. It rationalizes a harmful status quo rather than contributing meaningfully to resolving the underlying issue.
