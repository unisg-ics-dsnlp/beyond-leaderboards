The argument is weak from an eco-justice perspective. While acknowledging that bottled water is wasteful is a start, the rebuttal — "bottles can be reused" — is a superficial deflection that:

- **Ignores systemic harm**: Single-use plastic production itself causes ecological damage, regardless of reuse potential. Most bottles are *not* reused in practice.

- **Misses intergenerational justice**: Microplastic contamination and ecosystem degradation are burdens being shifted to future generations.
