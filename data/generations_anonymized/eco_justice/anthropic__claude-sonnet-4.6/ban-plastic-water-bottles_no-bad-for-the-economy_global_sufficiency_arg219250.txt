**Rating: 1 (Low)**

The argument is globally insufficient. While acknowledging reuse is a minor concession, it fails to rebut the major objections:

- **Production impact**: Manufacturing plastic bottles still consumes fossil fuels and generates emissions — reuse doesn't erase that.
- **Systemic waste**: Most bottles are *not* reused in practice; the argument relies on ideal behavior, not reality.
- **Commons exploitation**: Bottled water commodifies a shared resource, often depleting local aquifers.
- **Intergenerational burden**: Plastic pollution persists for centuries — reuse delays but doesn't eliminate this harm.

The reuse point is too narrow to constitute a sufficient defense of bottled water against its ecological and justice-based critiques.
