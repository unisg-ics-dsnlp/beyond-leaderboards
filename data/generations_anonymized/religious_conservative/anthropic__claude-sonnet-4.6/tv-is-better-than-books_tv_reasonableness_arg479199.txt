The argument makes some reasonable points — accessibility for those with sensory impairments is a genuinely considerate observation, and the currency of information is a fair practical concern. These contributions are relevant and broadly acceptable.

However, the argument overstates its case. Books carry the accumulated wisdom of generations — a treasure of inherited knowledge and moral formation that television rarely replicates.
