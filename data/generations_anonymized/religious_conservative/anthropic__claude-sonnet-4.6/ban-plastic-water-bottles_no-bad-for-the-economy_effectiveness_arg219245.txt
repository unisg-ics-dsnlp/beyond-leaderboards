**Rating: 2 (Medium)**

The argument presents concrete statistics from a credible source (Beverage Marketing Corporation) and conveys the sheer scale of the bottled water industry effectively. The numbers are specific and carry some persuasive weight.

A virtuous society must ask not merely *what is profitable*, but *what serves the common good*.

The argument lacks:
- A clear thesis it is trying to persuade us of
- Any appeal to duty, justice, or moral consequence
- Emotional or principled grounding
