I rate this argument **2 (Medium)**.

The premise that bottles *can* be reused is individually acceptable — it is factually true and aligns with the principle of stewardship over creation, which I hold dear. The relevance to the conclusion (countering the waste concern) is reasonable.

However, the argument is only partially sufficient. It acknowledges the waste problem but offers a behavioral solution that depends on individual virtue and discipline — which, while admirable, is not guaranteed. The sufficiency is weakened because reuse is possible but not assured in practice. A stronger argument would address *why* and *how* reuse would be reliably practiced.

Hence, **medium cogency** — sound in principle, but insufficiently developed.
