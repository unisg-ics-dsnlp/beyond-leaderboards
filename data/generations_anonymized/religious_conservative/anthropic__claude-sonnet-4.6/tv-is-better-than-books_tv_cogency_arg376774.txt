**Rating: 1 (Low)**

The argument is poorly cogent. The premise — that TV allows one to "explore the world from a couch" — is **individually weak** from a natural law and virtue perspective. Passive consumption is not genuine exploration; it substitutes vicarious experience for real engagement with creation, community, and duty.

Furthermore, the premise is **insufficient** to establish that TV is *better* (better than what? for what purpose?). No standard of the good is offered. A cogent argument requires grounding in *why* something is beneficial — for the person, the family, the community.

From my worldview, an argument praising sedentary passivity as a virtue actually **cuts against** human flourishing, which requires active participation in family, faith, and civic life — not couch-bound detachment.
