**Rating: 2 (Medium)**

From a materialist standpoint, the argument is a simple product promotion. The premise (Foxtel Go app exists on iPad) is **acceptable** as a factual claim, and it is **relevant** to the conclusion (watch TV anywhere, anytime).

However, it is only **partially sufficient** — it omits critical conditions: subscription cost, internet access, device ownership, and geographic restrictions. These are not trivial omissions, as they reflect **class barriers** to access — not every worker can afford an iPad, a data plan, *and* a Foxtel subscription simultaneously.

The argument functions adequately as a basic claim but lacks the sufficiency to be fully cogent, hence **Medium**.
