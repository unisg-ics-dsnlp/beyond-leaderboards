The argument attempts to counter the premise that bottled water is wasteful by pointing to bottle reusability. However, the premises are insufficient to support this conclusion because:

- The single premise (bottles *can* be reused) does not adequately address the **systemic waste** embedded in bottled water production — the energy, raw materials, and labor exploited in manufacturing millions of plastic bottles remain unaddressed.

- The argument ignores the broader **commodity logic** driving bottled water production — it exists to extract profit, not to serve collective need, making reuse a marginal fix to a structural problem.
