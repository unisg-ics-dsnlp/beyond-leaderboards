The statistics cited (Beverage Marketing Corporation figures) are **individually acceptable** as premises from an established industry source.

However, the argument suffers from **structural weaknesses**:

- **No clear conclusion** is explicitly stated — the data floats without a definitive claim being argued for

But cogency requires more than raw statistics; it demands a logical chain from premises to conclusion.

**Medium cogency** — the evidence is acceptable and relevant, but the argument lacks sufficient structure and a clear conclusion to rate higher.
