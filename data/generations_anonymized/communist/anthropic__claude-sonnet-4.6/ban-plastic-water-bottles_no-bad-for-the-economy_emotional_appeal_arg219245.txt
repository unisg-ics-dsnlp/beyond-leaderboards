**Rating: 1 (Low)**

This passage is composed almost entirely of dry statistical data — revenue figures, production volumes, growth percentages. From any analytical standpoint, including a Marxist one, it presents the **raw facts of capitalist commodity expansion** without framing them in a way that stirs emotional response.

There is no appeal to human suffering, no invocation of solidarity, no narrative of exploitation. The numbers alone — however staggering — do not emotionally mobilize the reader. They inform, but they do not *move*.

A truly effective emotional appeal would connect these billions in profit to the **workers exploited in production** or the **communities denied access to clean water** — but this argument does neither.
